# Workast

![Workast](assets/logo.png)

Organize work with your team and agents. Manage tasks, projects, and documents in the spaces your team already uses.

## Use it

Add the plugin, then sign in to Workast when asked. Ask for a task, a meeting follow-up, or a report. The work is created and updated in your Workast account.

## Data

The plugin sends the requests you make to your Workast account through https://mcp.workast.com/mcp. Sign-in uses your Workast account. The plugin stores nothing itself.
